## Recent hackathon work

### [PawTry Together](https://github.com/Hye-Seung-Kim/petfit)
*Google Fashion Hackathon · 2026*

A shared virtual fitting room for pets. A host streams a dog video through the Vonage Video API, captures a frame, and uses Google Gemini image editing to preview an outfit. Friends in the same room can see new looks, vote, and help choose the final outfit.

`Google Gemini` `Vonage Video API` `Node.js` `Express` `JavaScript`

[Repository](https://github.com/Hye-Seung-Kim/petfit) · [Live demo](https://petfit-livid.vercel.app)

### [VisionLog](https://github.com/Hye-Seung-Kim/visionlog)
*IBM AI Builders Challenge · 2026*

A vision-change tracking and medical handoff prototype for long-duration space missions. It combines structured astronaut check-ins, deterministic safety rules, NASA evidence and environmental data, and IBM Granite explanations grounded only in verified records.

`IBM Granite` `FastAPI` `TypeScript` `Python` `NASA OSDR`

[Repository](https://github.com/Hye-Seung-Kim/visionlog) · [Demo video](https://youtu.be/lBuZkpN74yc)

### [Data Patrol](https://github.com/candpixie/data-patrol-dell-hack)
*Dell x NVIDIA AI Hackathon · 2026*

A local-first fact-freshness engine for sensitive business data. The first workflow checks vendor invoices against contracts, keeps the evidence traceable, and routes uncertain cases to human review. The demo runs local inference on NVIDIA GB10 hardware without sending private documents to a remote model.

`NVIDIA GB10` `MongoDB` `FastAPI` `TypeScript` `Python`

[Team repository](https://github.com/candpixie/data-patrol-dell-hack) · [Demo video](https://youtu.be/ihl8WF_ngKw)
